# Little Crossword — Classic clue sources

The 100 Classic mini crossword answer grids are generated locally from word
frequencies using the open-source Python TextBlob package's English spelling
dataset. No paid API or user account is needed to play the crosswords.

The majority of clue and hint definitions in `data/classics100.json` are
adapted from **English Wiktionary**, accessed in machine-readable form through
[mhollingshead/open-dictionary](https://github.com/mhollingshead/open-dictionary).

Source: https://en.wiktionary.org/
Wiktionary license: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Wiktionary content is authored by Wiktionary contributors, whose histories are
available on each individual word-entry page. Its licensing and attribution
requirements apply to the adapted definitions in this crossword collection.

Several words use older custom clues or basic wordplay fallback clues where
usable dictionary senses were not available. These autogenerated fallback clues
have not all been manually edited for playability and may require improvements.

The original 24 hand-written Practice puzzles and the twelve monthly topic
features are maintained separately from this new 100-puzzle collection.
